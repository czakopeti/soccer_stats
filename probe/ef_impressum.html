This helps me improve the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes data such as a shortened IP address, device and browser information, the pages visited and the time of the visit. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses.

Google Analytics is used only with your consent (§ 25 (1) TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you give or refuse in the same consent banner as for advertising.
